Trip highlights:
- Visit to the UNESCO World Heritage Site of **Barumini**, the most complex nuraghe in Sardinia
- Visit to the Phoenician city of **Nora**
- **Sardinian honey** presentation and tasting
- Option to attend an evening performance at the **Teatro Lirico di Cagliari**
- **Private boat trip** along the stunning coastline around Alghero
