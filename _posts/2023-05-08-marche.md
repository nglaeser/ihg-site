Trip highlights:
- Guided tours of the main cities and towns, meals of finely prepared regional dishes, and tastings of local products
- **Nature walking tour** in the Conero Regional Park with a certified naturalist guide
- Sensorial experience with an **artisan shoemaker**
- "Aperitivo in musica": half-day event with live traditional music, dances, and a local meal
- Farewell party with in a **medieval hamlet** nestled in the woods around an 11th century watchtower
