Trip highlights:
- Hands-on experience at the **Lavazza coffee museum**
- **Cooking class** during which we’ll create (and enjoy!) a 3-course dinner
- Visit to the **Langhe wine region**, a UNESCO World Heritage Site
- Optional expedition on the **panoramic SkyWay** to a summit near Mont Blanc, the highest peak in Western Europe
- **Private cruise** of Lake Maggiore with visit to the islets of Isola Bella and Isola Madre
