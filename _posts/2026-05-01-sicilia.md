Trip highlights:
- The ancient Phoenician and Greek cities of **Palermo** and **Syracuse**, including visits to noble palaces and architectural landmarks
- Experience **authentic Sicilian street food and dishes** like cannoli, cassata, and arancine
- A **Pupi performance**, a puppet theater featuring Sicily’s traditional marionnettes
- Taste and make your own **Modica chocolate** based on an ancient Arabic method
- A **private boat trip** along one of the most picturesque coastlines of Italy
- A day at a countryside villa immersed in **Sicilian traditions** accompanied by live music and food
- **Farewell dinner** with live music
