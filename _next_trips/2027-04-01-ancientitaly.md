Trip highlights:
- Explore the extraordinary Etruscan necropolises of Tarquinia and Cerveteri, UNESCO World Heritage Sites offering a unique glimpse into one of Italy’s earliest civilizations 
- Hadrian’s Villa and Villa d’Este in Tivoli, masterpieces of Roman architecture and Renaissance garden design 
- Discover the medieval beauty of Viterbo, Orvieto, Perugia, and Siena, with their cathedrals, historic centers, and artistic treasures 
- Experience the spiritual atmosphere of Assisi, home of St. Francis and some of Italy’s finest medieval frescoes 
- Enjoy the rolling hills, vineyards, and timeless villages of the Val d’Orcia and Renaissance Pienza 
- Visit the fascinating Park of the Monsters in Bomarzo, one of the most imaginative and unusual gardens of the Italian Renaissance 
- Enjoy authentic regional cuisine, local wines, chocolate tasting in Perugia, and traditional Tuscan and Umbrian hospitality 
- Farewell dinner celebrating the history, beauty, and flavors of Central Italy
