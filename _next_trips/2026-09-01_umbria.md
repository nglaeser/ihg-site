Trip highlights:
- Discover **Pienza**, UNESCO World Heritage Site, built in the 1500s on the principles of Renaissance urban planning
- Taste local specialties, including Pecorino cheese, Brunello wine, Umbrian olive oil, and artisanal chocolate
- Sail on **Lake Trasimeno** and enjoy lunch on **Isola Maggiore**
- Visit iconic **Tuscan and Umbrian towns** including Montepulciano, Siena, Arezzo, and Perugia, and discover some of their less-known corners
- **Farewell dinner** with live music

All of our tours include stays in 4-star or 4-star equivalent hotels, visits led by certified tour guides, and the 24/7 services of our tour managers Laura & Livia to ensure a smooth and pleasant trip.
