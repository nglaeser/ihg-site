Hear what our guests have to say

Our small tour sizes allow a level of attention to our guests’ needs and desires that is just not possible with a larger group. Our groups are limited to 14 travelers so we can listen to every one of our guests before and during each trip, creating the best possible experience. This allows us to find unexpected little gems, organize customized events based on your interests and passions, and choose boutique hotels and delicious gourmet restaurants that don’t accept larger groups.

That is really where our passion lies: surprising and delighting our guests with events and experiences carefully created just for them, and to help them discover the true heart of Italy.
